Car Maintenance Diary - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 06/30/2026

DRodero Mobile Apps ("us", "we", or "our") is committed to protecting and respecting the privacy of the users of Car Maintenance Diary (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"). This Privacy Policy outlines in a clear and transparent manner how information is handled within the Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. All data you enter into the Application regarding your car's maintenance (such as mileage, service logs, expenses, dates, and history) is processed and stored exclusively on your own Android device's local storage. We do not operate external servers, making it technically impossible for us to access, view, or manipulate your records.

2. Device Permissions and Backups

For the proper functioning of the Application's features, the following access may be required:

Storage / Files Access: This permission is requested solely and exclusively when you voluntarily choose to use the Export or Import data feature. It is required to save the backup file (e.g., in JSON or CSV format) to a folder of your choice on your device, or to read it if you wish to restore your information.

The control, custody, and safeguarding of this backup file remain the sole responsibility of the user.

3. Data Security and Loss

Since all data resides locally on your phone, the security of your information directly depends on the security of your own device. Please note that if you uninstall the Application or reset your phone without previously exporting a backup, all your maintenance records will be permanently deleted. Because we do not maintain cloud backups, we will not be able to help you recover your history.

4. Third-Party Services

This Application is a clean environment. It does not integrate third-party analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics) or advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). No external entity collects data about your usage of the Application.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect, for example, changes to the app's features or for legal reasons. We advise you to review this page periodically. Any modifications become effective immediately upon publication.
