# Car Maintenance Diary - Public Repository

## 📋 About

**Car Maintenance Diary** is a mobile application designed to help users record and manage their car maintenance history. It provides a simple and effective way to track mileage, service logs, expenses, dates, and maintenance history directly on your Android device.

## 🔍 Key Features

- **Local Data Storage**: All your maintenance records are stored exclusively on your device
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect any personally identifiable information
- **Export & Import**: Backup your data in JSON or CSV format
- **Clean Environment**: No third-party analytics or advertising networks
- **Multi-language**: Available in English and Spanish
